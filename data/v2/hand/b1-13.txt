@create style=detailed domain=b1_goat_partnership
me and my cousin raise goats together as partners. all feed and medicine expenses are paid by either of us, and when we sell, the money is split half and half. show what each of us has paid, and who should pay whom at the end
---
app "Goat Partners" paw mint
table partner_p name text
table gexpense partner_p ref partner_p, amount money, what text?
table gsale amount money, animal text?
let all_cost = sum(gexpense.amount)
let profit_each = (sum(gsale.amount) - all_cost) / 2
"Profit to split" sum(gsale.amount) - all_cost big
each partner_p sort name as p
  row card tap edit p
    p.name bold grow
    text "paid" sum(p.gexpense.amount)
    text "gets" round(sum(p.gexpense.amount) + profit_each) bold
form gexpense, "Expense"
form gsale, "Sale"
form partner_p, "Add partner"
===
@create style=typos domain=b1_buffalo_loan_chit
i took cash from 3 peple to buy a buffalo and return little by little, who i owe and how much is left
---
app "Buffalo Loan" money slate
table lender name text
table taken lender ref lender, amount money
table returned2 lender ref lender, amount money
each lender sort name as l
  let left = sum(l.taken.amount) - sum(l.returned2.amount)
  row card tap edit l
    l.name bold grow
    text left bold color if(left > 0, warn, good)
"I owe in total" sum(taken.amount) - sum(returned2.amount) big
form taken, "Took money"
form returned2, "Returned"
form lender, "Add lender"
