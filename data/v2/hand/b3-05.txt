@create style=custom_ui domain=b3_antakshari
antakshari teams, tap a team's card to give it 10 points, big totals, undo button
---
app "Antakshari" music plum
table team name text
table point team ref team, pts num 1..50 = 10
each team sort sum(it.point.pts) desc as t
  stack card gap 1 tap add point team = t
    row
      t.name head grow
      sum(t.point.pts) huge
    "Tap the card for 10 points" faint
button "Undo last point" tap remove last(point)
form team, "Add team"
===
@create style=custom_ui domain=b3_penalty_shootout
penalty shootout between red and blue, buttons for scores and misses for each side, big goal counts and a dot for every kick (green goal, red miss), new shootout button
---
app "Penalty Shootout" ball ink
table kick team pick Red|Blue = Red, scored bool
let red_goals = count(kick where team = Red and scored = yes)
let blue_goals = count(kick where team = Blue and scored = yes)
row gap 2 align center
  stack grow align center
    "Red" faint
    red_goals huge
  stack grow align center
    "Blue" faint
    blue_goals huge
row gap 1
  button "Red scores" fill red tap add kick team = Red, scored = yes
  button "Red misses" tap add kick team = Red, scored = no
row gap 1
  button "Blue scores" fill blue tap add kick team = Blue, scored = yes
  button "Blue misses" tap add kick team = Blue, scored = no
row gap 1
  each kick sort at as k
    box width 20 height 20 radius 10 fill if(k.scored, good, bad)
button "New shootout" tap remove all kick
===
@create style=detailed domain=b3_poker_night
Home poker with friends. Each player has buy-ins and cash-outs recorded separately, and I want to see each player's net win or loss in green or red, and whether the money in and money out match.
---
app "Poker Night" cards slate
table player name text
table buyin player ref player, amount money
table cashout player ref player, amount money
each player sort name as p
  let net = sum(p.cashout.amount) - sum(p.buyin.amount)
  row card
    stack grow
      p.name bold
      "In" sum(p.buyin.amount) "| out" sum(p.cashout.amount) faint
    net big color if(net >= 0, good, bad)
"Still on the table" sum(buyin.amount) - sum(cashout.amount) faint
form player, "Add player"
form buyin, "Add buy-in"
form cashout, "Add cash-out"
