@create style=detailed domain=b1_margin_products
in my general store each product has a cost price and a selling price. I enter units sold daily and want profit per product this month and the product earning the most
---
app "Margins" tag ocean
table product name text, cost money, price money
table units product ref product, qty num 1..500
let best = max(product, sum(it.units.qty month) * (it.price - it.cost))
"Best product profit" best big
each product sort name as p
  let profit = sum(p.units.qty month) * (p.price - p.cost)
  row card tap edit p
    p.name bold grow
    text sum(p.units.qty month) "sold"
    profit bold color if(profit = best, good, ink)
form units, "Sold today"
form product, "Add product"
===

@create style=multi_feature domain=b1_used_phones

---

table handset_repair handset ref handset, cost money, what text?

    else
      "in stock" color warn
