@create style=detailed domain=b1_rusk_dispatch
I supply rusks and cakes to 8 shops. every morning I dispatch packs, next day unsold packs come back. show for each shop packs dispatched, returned and net sold with money at a rate I set
---
app "Rusk Supply" bread ocean
table shop name text, area text?
table dispatch shop ref shop, packs num 1..500
table returned shop ref shop, packs num 1..500
setting pack_rate money = 90 "Rate per pack"
each shop sort name as s
  let net = sum(s.dispatch.packs month) - sum(s.returned.packs month)
  row card tap edit s
    s.name bold grow
    text sum(s.dispatch.packs month) "out"
    text sum(s.returned.packs month) "back"
    text net * pack_rate bold
"Net packs this month" sum(dispatch.packs month) - sum(returned.packs month) big
form dispatch, "Dispatch"
form returned, "Returned"
form shop, "Add shop"
===
@create style=multi_feature domain=b1_newspaper_hawker
newspaper hawker with about 60 homes, each home takes a paper and pays monthly. I tick the holiday when a home skipped and the bill is days delivered times the price of that paper
---
app "Hawker" note slate
table homepaper name text, paper pick Dawn|Jang|Express|Business = Dawn, price money = 60
table skipped homepaper ref homepaper
table paidmonth homepaper ref homepaper, amount money
state m date = today
monthnav m
each homepaper sort name as h
  let days_taken = monthdays(m) - count(h.skipped month of m)
  let bill = days_taken * h.price
  row card tap edit h
    stack grow
      h.name bold
      text h.paper faint
    text days_taken "days"
    bill bold color if(sum(h.paidmonth.amount month of m) >= bill, good, ink)
    toggle h.skipped on m
form paidmonth, "Paid"
form homepaper, "Add home"
===
