@create style=detailed domain=b4_car_pool
four of us share one car to the office. whoever drives that day buys fuel, and we split the total fuel cost by the number of rides each person took. show what each owes or gets back
---
app "Car Pool" car ocean

table ride_p rider_p ref rider_p

      r.name bold

form fuel_p, "Bought fuel"
form rider_p, "Add person"
