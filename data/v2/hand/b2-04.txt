@create style=detailed domain=b2_archery_rounds
Our archery club shoots rounds of 10 arrows. For every arrow I enter the points (0 to 10). I want each archer's total for a round, their best round ever, and the club record at the top.
---
app "Archery" target mint
table archer name text
table round archer ref archer, label text
table arrow round ref round, points num 0..10
"Club record" max(round, sum(it.arrow.points)) big
each archer sort name as a
  stack card gap 1
    a.name head
    "Best round" max(a.round, sum(it.arrow.points)) bold
    each a.round sort at desc top 3 as r
      row gap 1
        r.label grow
        sum(r.arrow.points) "pts" bold
        count(r.arrow) "arrows" faint
form archer, "Add archer"
form round, "Start round"
form arrow, "Add arrow"
===
