@create style=detailed domain=b1_cattle_feed
cattle feed bags: I buy feed bags and every day I note how many bags the animals eat. tell me how many bags are left and how many days they will last at this week's usage
---
app "Feed Stock" package mint
table bought bags num 1..500, cost money
table eaten bags num 0.25..20
let in_stock = sum(bought.bags) - sum(eaten.bags)
let daily_use = sum(eaten.bags last 7 days) / 7
in_stock "bags left" big color if(in_stock < 5, warn, ink)
"Lasts about" if(daily_use > 0, floor(in_stock / daily_use), 0) "days" head
daybars last 7 days, sum(eaten.bags on it)
quick eaten, bags, [1, 2, 3]
form bought, "Bought bags"
list bought by day
===
