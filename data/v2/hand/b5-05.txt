@create style=detailed domain=b5_stable_horses
small horse stable. each horse has a feed amount in kg a day, farrier visits and vet visits are logged. farrier is due every 60 days and I want the horses that are due on top
---
app "Stable" paw slate
table horse name text, feed_kg num 1..20 = 6
table farrier horse ref horse, cost money?
table vetvisit horse ref horse, reason text
"Feed per day" sum(horse.feed_kg) "kg" big
each horse sort name as h
  if due(h.farrier, 60)
    row card tap edit h
      h.name bold grow
      "farrier due" color warn
each horse sort name as h
  row card tap edit h
    h.name bold grow
    plural(since(h.farrier), "day") "since farrier" faint
    count(h.vetvisit) "vet" faint
form farrier, "Farrier came"
form vetvisit, "Vet visit"
form horse, "Add horse"
===
