@create style=detailed domain=b2_hive_checks
I keep eight beehives on the roof. Each week I open them and note the strength (weak, ok, strong). Honey harvest is in kg per hive. Show the strong hives count, the honey total this year and honey per hive.
---
app "Hive log" leaf mint
table hive label text, queen_year num 2015..2030
table inspection hive ref hive, strength pick Weak|Ok|Strong = Ok, note text?
table harvest hive ref hive, kg num 0.1..60
"Honey this year" sum(harvest.kg year) "kg" big
count(hive, last(it.inspection.strength) = Strong) "strong hives" faint
each hive sort label as h
  row card tap edit h
    h.label bold grow
    last(h.inspection.strength) color if(last(h.inspection.strength) = Weak, bad, ink)
    sum(h.harvest.kg) "kg" faint
form hive, "Add hive"
form inspection, "Inspection"
form harvest, "Harvest"
===
