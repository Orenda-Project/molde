@create style=detailed domain=b2_physio_plan
After my knee operation the physio gave me four exercises to do daily. I want to tick each one when done today, see my streak, and log a pain score from 1 to 10 each evening to see if it is improving.
---
app "Knee rehab" heart mint
table exercise name text, reps text = "10 x 3"
table done exercise ref exercise
table pain score num 1..10
"Done today" count(done today) "of" count(exercise) big
each exercise as e
  row card
    toggle e.done
    stack grow
      e.name bold
      e.reps faint
    streak(e.done) "days" faint
"Pain over two weeks" head
trend pain last 14 days, it.score
form pain, "Log pain"
form exercise, "Add exercise"
===
