@create style=detailed domain=b1_shop_credit
my grocery shop gives stuff on credit to regular customers. I need to see how much each one owes me, and when they pay something it should bring the number down. cash sales should not count as owed
---
app "Shop Khata" wallet slate
table customer name text, phone text?
table sale customer ref customer, amount money, kind pick Credit|Cash = Credit, item text?
table payment customer ref customer, amount money
let owed = sum(sale.amount where kind = Credit) - sum(payment.amount)
"Total owed to the shop" owed big
each customer sort name as c
  let balance = sum(c.sale.amount where kind = Credit) - sum(c.payment.amount)
  row card tap edit c
    c.name bold grow
    balance bold color if(balance > 0, bad, good)
form customer, "Add customer"
form sale, "Record sale"
form payment, "Record payment"
===
