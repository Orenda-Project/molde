@create style=detailed domain=b5_radio_ads
local FM station ad log. clients buy spots with a length in seconds on a day. we charge by the 10 seconds at a rate that is a setting. show money billed this month per client and total
---
app "Ad Log" music plum
table advertiser name text
table spot advertiser ref advertiser, day date, secs num 10..120 = 30
setting rate_10s money = 400 "Rate per 10 seconds"
let cost(s spot) = s.secs / 10 * rate_10s
"Billed this month" sum(spot month, cost(it)) big
each advertiser sort name as a
  row card tap edit a
    a.name bold grow
    count(a.spot month) "spots" faint
    sum(a.spot month, cost(it)) bold
form spot, "Book a spot"
form advertiser, "Add client"
===
@create style=custom_ui domain=b5_toll_booth
bridge toll booth. every vehicle that passes is logged as bike, car or truck, fees 20, 50 and 150. one big button for each type, and a chart of vehicles by hour of the day
---
app "Toll Booth" bus slate
table passage kind pick Bike|Car|Truck = Car
let fee(k text) = if(k = Bike, 20, if(k = Car, 50, 150))
row gap 1
  each options passage.kind as k
    button k tap add passage kind = k
"Collected today" sum(passage today, fee(it.kind)) big
plot 0..24, 0..max(1..24, count(passage today where hour(at) = it)) axes
  each 0..23
    rect it + 0.1, 0, 0.8, count(passage today where hour(at) = it) fill accent radius 2
    tick it + 0.5, it
===
@create style=multi_feature domain=b5_vaccine_fridge
pharmacy vaccine fridges, I write the temperature twice a day for each fridge. anything outside 2 to 8 degrees is bad. draw the line with the safe range as gridlines and count bad readings this week
---
app "Cold Chain" snow ocean
table fridge name text
table reading fridge ref fridge, celsius num -10..30
each fridge sort name as f
  stack card gap 1
    row
      f.name bold grow
      last(f.reading.celsius) "C" bold color if(last(f.reading.celsius) < 2 or last(f.reading.celsius) > 8, bad, good)
    "Out of range this week" count(f.reading week where celsius < 2 or celsius > 8) faint
    plot 0..max(count(f.reading), 2), -2..12 axes
      poly stroke accent thick 2
        each f.reading sort at
          pt index, it.celsius
form reading, "Add reading"
form fridge, "Add fridge"
===

@edit style=plain domain=b5_radio_discount ref=b5_radio_ads
give 10 percent off when a client books more than 5 spots in a month, make the percent a setting
---
~ setting rate_10s money = 400 => setting rate_10s money = 400\nsetting bulk_discount num = 10 "Bulk discount %"
~ sum(a.spot month, cost(it)) bold => sum(a.spot month, cost(it)) * if(count(a.spot month) > 5, 100 - bulk_discount, 100) / 100 bold
