@create style=multi_feature domain=b4_kitchen_staff
restaurant kitchen has 5 staff with hourly wage. each shift has start and end time, total hours is the difference. show the weekly wage per person and for the whole kitchen
---
app "Kitchen Staff" users slate
table cook name text, hourly money
table shift cook ref cook, day date, start time, end time
each cook sort name as c
  let hrs = sum(c.shift week, (it.end - it.start) / 1h)
  row card tap edit c
    stack grow
      c.name bold
      text fixed(hrs, 1) "hours this week" faint
    text round(hrs * c.hourly) bold
text "Kitchen wages this week" round(sum(shift week, (it.end - it.start) / 1h * it.cook.hourly)) big
form shift, "Add shift"
form cook, "Add cook"
===
@edit style=plain domain=b4_staff_overtime ref=b4_kitchen_staff
overtime after 40 hours a week is paid one and a half
---
~ round(hrs * c.hourly) bold => text round(min(hrs, 40) * c.hourly + max(hrs - 40, 0) * c.hourly * 1.5) bold
===
