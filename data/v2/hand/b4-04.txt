@create style=multi_feature domain=b4_fish_market
fish seller at the market. each morning i buy fish by kg from the boat at one rate and sell at another, some spoils. per fish type show kg bought, sold, spoiled and the profit
---
app "Fish Stall" fish ocean
table fish name text, buy_rate money, sell_rate money
table bought fish ref fish, kg num 1..500
table soldf fish ref fish, kg num 0.2..100
table spoiled fish ref fish, kg num 0.2..100
each fish sort name as f
  let gain = sum(f.soldf.kg) * f.sell_rate - sum(f.bought.kg) * f.buy_rate
  stack card gap 1 tap edit f
    row
      f.name head grow
      text gain bold color if(gain >= 0, good, bad)
    text "in stock" sum(f.bought.kg) - sum(f.soldf.kg) - sum(f.spoiled.kg) "kg" faint
    text "spoiled" sum(f.spoiled.kg) "kg" tiny color warn
bars fish sort name, sum(it.spoiled.kg), it.name
form bought, "Bought"
form soldf, "Sold"
form spoiled, "Spoiled"
form fish, "New fish"
===
