@create style=detailed domain=b4_supplier_purchases
the restaurant buys from a few suppliers, butcher, vegetable man, dairy, gas agency. every purchase has the supplier, a category and the amount. show total bought this month per supplier and total for all
---
app "Purchases" cart slate
table supplier name text, phone text?
table buy supplier ref supplier, category pick Meat|Veg|Dairy|Gas|Other = Meat, amount money
text "Bought this month" sum(buy.amount month) big
each supplier sort name as s
  row card tap edit s
    stack grow
      s.name bold
      text count(s.buy month) "purchases" faint
    text sum(s.buy.amount month) bold
form buy, "New purchase"
form supplier, "Add supplier"
===
@edit style=plain domain=b4_purchase_payments ref=b4_supplier_purchases

---
~ table buy supplier ref supplier, category => table paid_s supplier ref supplier, amount money\ntable buy supplier ref supplier, category
~ sum(s.buy.amount month) bold => text sum(s.buy.amount) - sum(s.paid_s.amount) "owed" bold
+ form paid_s, "Paid supplier"
===
