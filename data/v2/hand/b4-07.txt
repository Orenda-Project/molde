@create style=detailed domain=b4_peak_flow
my son has asthma. morning and evening he blows into a peak flow meter, the number goes in. his personal best is 350. above 80 percent of it is green, 50 to 80 yellow, below 50 red. show the colour of the last reading and a line for the week
---
app "Peak Flow" heart ocean
table blow pf num 50..800, moment pick Morning|Evening = Morning
