@create style=detailed domain=b5_birthday_party
planning my daughter's birthday party. guests with a yes, no or maybe, how many kids each is bringing, and gifts received with who gave it. total heads on top because cake slices depend on it
---
app "Party Plan" gift plum
table guest name text, rsvp pick Yes|No|Maybe = Maybe, kids num 0..8 = 1
table gift guest ref guest, what text
"Heads coming" sum(guest where rsvp = Yes, it.kids + 1) big
"Maybe" sum(guest where rsvp = Maybe, it.kids + 1) faint
each guest sort name as g
  row card tap edit g
    g.name bold grow
    g.rsvp color if(g.rsvp = Yes, good, if(g.rsvp = No, bad, warn))
    count(g.gift) "gifts" faint
form guest, "Add guest"
form gift, "Gift received"
===
