@create style=detailed domain=b2_class_marks
I teach three sections and give a quiz every Friday. I need each student's marks against their name, the class average, and a red flag on anyone averaging under 40.
---
app "Class marks" school ocean
table student name text, section pick Red|Blue|Green = Red
table mark student ref student, subject pick Maths|Science|English = Maths, score num 0..100
"Class average" round(avg(mark.score)) big
count(student, avg(it.mark.score) < 40) "students under 40" faint
each student sort name as s
  row card tap edit s swipe remove s
    s.name bold grow
    s.section faint
    round(avg(s.mark.score)) bold color if(avg(s.mark.score) < 40, bad, ink)
form student, "Add student"
form mark, "Add mark"
===
