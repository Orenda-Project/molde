@create style=detailed domain=b1_rice_mill
rice mill: farmers bring paddy in maunds and later I give back milled rice and keep a share as my charge. for each farmer show paddy brought, rice returned and the balance still with me
---
app "Rice Mill" bread slate
table farmer name text, village text?
table paddy farmer ref farmer, maunds num 1..500
table rice_back farmer ref farmer, maunds num 1..500
each farmer sort name as f
  let held = sum(f.paddy.maunds) - sum(f.rice_back.maunds)
  row card tap edit f
    f.name bold grow
    text sum(f.paddy.maunds) "in"
    text sum(f.rice_back.maunds) "out"
    held bold color if(held > 0, warn, good)
"Paddy in the mill" sum(paddy.maunds) - sum(rice_back.maunds) big
form paddy, "Paddy received"
form rice_back, "Rice returned"
form farmer, "Add farmer"
===
