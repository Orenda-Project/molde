@create style=multi_feature domain=h2a_shop
small shop with two tabs. Stock tab where items under their low level turn red-ish and I can edit them, and a Sell tab where tapping an item sells one and lowers stock. total sales too
---
app "Shop" cart slate
table item name text, qty num 0.., low num 0.. = 5, price money
table sale item ref item, n num 1.. = 1
screen stock "Stock" box
  form item
  each item sort it.name
    row card fill if(it.qty <= it.low, warn, soft) tap edit it
      text it.name grow
      text it.qty bold
screen sell "Sell" cart
  stat sum(sale, it.n * it.item.price), "total sales"
  each item
    button it.name it.price tap add sale item = it then set it.qty = it.qty - 1
===
