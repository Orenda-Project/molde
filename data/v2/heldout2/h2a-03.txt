@create style=custom_ui domain=h2a_emi
loan installment calculator, amount, yearly interest rate and months, shows monthly payment and total
---
app "Loan" coin slate
setting amount money = 500000 "Loan amount"
setting rate num 0..100 = 12 "Yearly rate %"
setting months num 1..360 = 24 "Months"
let r = rate / 1200
let emi = if(r = 0, amount / months, amount * r * pow(1 + r, months) / (pow(1 + r, months) - 1))
stat emi, "per month"
stat emi * months, "total to pay"
stat emi * months - amount, "interest"
===

@create style=multi_feature domain=h2a_sleep
sleep tracker. bedtime and wake time each night, hours slept shown as a line, and a goal of 8 hours with average on a second tab. remind me at 22:00 to go to bed
