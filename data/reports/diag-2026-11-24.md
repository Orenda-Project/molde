# Molde diagnosis, 2026-11-24 (after Kamal's phone test)

Model: r2 Gemma 270M Q4_K_M through the app's own decode loop (arm E). App: 0.2.0 x86_64 on the emulator, head 49ca93b. Evidence files are in this folder.

## Summary

More training on the same kind of data will not fix it. Causes, ranked:

1. **Ask loop (data + app).** v2 labels short requests as ask (1 word 95% ask, 2 words 73%), including 143 asks that name a thing ("budget", "notes"). The app sends only the latest message after an ask, so "Water" reaches the model as a new one-word request; no cap on asks; options are plain text. Measured: 80% of one- or two-word named requests ask first; after answering the way the app does, 27 of 32 ask again. Emulator: "todo" -> asked about shopping -> "Save" -> asked about savings -> built a Savings app.
2. **Generic look (data).** Training targets: median 11 lines, 2.8% with two or more custom pieces, 0% app bar, 77% always-open form. Demo apps (hand-written): median 42 lines, 93% custom, 53% app bar. The model copies its training look. The phone draws a demo program identically to the web demo (same CSS); the demo looked richer partly because it was pre-filled with weeks of sample data.
3. **Small changes fail (data).** 11 of 40 correct (1 of 20 on long demo programs). No training edit has a program over 38 lines; on long programs the model drops whole sections (Water + "make it green" deleted the 7-day chart). Reorder never taught; "bigger" 11 pairs; goal changes with `set` 15 pairs.
4. **App bugs.** Setting-only changes are reported as "nothing changed" (stored value beats new default unless `set`; `uiChanges()` skips settings). Chat defaults to "Change this app", resets after every create, so new-app requests are sent as changes and come back "already works like that". Phone context is 2048 tokens, too small to rewrite long programs.
5. **Language.** Only 5 themes (3 of them blue); no red, orange, pink, yellow; no dark theme from a program.
6. **Model size.** Lowest: where data covers a case, r2 does it.

## Bars for the fix tracks

- App: zero ask loops on 10 named requests (emulator script); New app / Change flows 10 of 10; a goal change shows on screen.
- Data v3: first reply is an ask on at most 10% of 30 named one- or two-word requests; at least 28 of 30 answered asks build; at least 36 of 40 small changes correct with at least 95% of other lines kept; blind judge prefers or ties the model app vs the hand-written demo on at least 10 of 15 requests.
- Language: "make it red/green/purple/dark" expressible and done in at least 9 of 10.
- Model: at least 90% valid on a new heldout with short requests and long-program edits.
