dry fruit shop. customers want a mixed box so i tap plus 100 grams on each nut they pick and the total price shows up. need to clear the basket, and when the customer pays tap sold so it counts in today's money. every nut has its own rate per kg

---

    stack grow gap 0
      n.name bold
      n.rate "per kg" faint
    sum(n.basket.grams) "g" bold
    button "+100 g" tap add basket nut = n, grams = 100
row gap 1
  button "Clear" tap remove all basket
  button "Sold" fill accent tap add sale amount = round(total) then remove all basket
"Sold today" sum(sale.amount today) head
