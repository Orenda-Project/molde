@rich domain=d2_tax_set_aside topic=freelancer_tax_set_aside style=detailed
as a freelancer I keep spending the tax money. log every payment I receive with client and amount, set my tax percent, and show how much to set aside this month and in total, and how much is really mine
needs: log a payment with client and amount | set the tax percent | amount to set aside this month | money that is really mine | chart of income per month
---
app "Tax jar" coin yellow
table pay client text, amount money
setting taxp num 0..60 = 20 "Tax percent"
topbar "Tax jar", pay, "Log payment"
statcard sum(pay.amount month) * taxp / 100, "to set aside from this month's" sum(pay.amount month)
statcard sum(pay.amount) * (100 - taxp) / 100, "is really yours after tax from" sum(pay.amount) "total"

    each 0..5 as k
      let d0 = addmonths(today, k - 5)
      rect k + 0.15, 0, 0.7, sum(pay.amount month of d0) fill accent radius 2
      tick k + 0.5, monthname(d0)
list pay by day, "No payments yet."
