needs: add a routine step | tick each step tonight | steps done tonight count | last seven nights as dots | reminder at 7:30
---
app "Bedtime" moon ink dark
table step name text
table did step ref step
let left = count(step, count(it.did today) = 0)
remind "Bedtime routine starts now" at 19:30
topbar "Bedtime", step, "Add step"
if left = 0 and count(step) > 0
  "All done. Sleep well." head color good
else
  left "steps left tonight" head
each step as s empty "No steps yet. Add bath, teeth, pyjamas, story."
  row card gap 2 tap edit s swipe remove s
    toggle s.did
    text s.name grow
row gap 1
  each days last 7 days as d
    stack grow gap 0 align center
      text dayname(d) tiny color muted
      box height 10 width 24 radius 5 fill if(count(step) > 0 and count(step, count(it.did on d) = 0) = 0, accent, soft)
===
@rich domain=r6_sibling_turns topic=sibling turn taking style=custom_ui
ali and sara fight over who picks the movie, the game or the front seat. choose what it is, tap who picked, and the app says whose turn is next because the other one picked less. history underneath
needs: choose movie, game or front seat | buttons for who picked | shows whose turn is next | counts of picks for each child | history of picks
---
app "Whose turn" smile orange
table pick who pick Ali|Sara, what pick Movie|Game|Front_seat = Movie
state w pick Movie|Game|Front_seat = Movie
let ali = count(pick where who = Ali and what = w)
let sara = count(pick where who = Sara and what = w)
choice w
stack card gap 1 align center
  text "Next to pick" faint
  if(ali <= sara, "Ali", "Sara") huge
  "Ali" ali "· Sara" sara faint
row gap 1
  button "Ali picked" grow tap add pick who = Ali, what = w
  button "Sara picked" grow tap add pick who = Sara, what = w
list pick top 10, "Nobody has picked yet."
