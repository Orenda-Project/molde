@rich domain=r7_book_year_pace topic=yearly book count and monthly pace style=detailed
my goal is 24 books this year. each time i finish one i log the title, pages and a rating. show how many books against the target, whether i am ahead or behind the pace for this month, books per month as bars and the average rating
needs: log a finished book with title, pages and rating | books against the yearly target | ahead or behind pace message | books per month bars | average rating
---
app "Book year" book purple
table finished title text, pages num 1..3000 = 250, rating num 1..5 = 4
setting yearly num = 24 "Books this year"
let should = round(yearly * month(today) / 12)
topbar "Book year", finished, "Log book"
statcard count(finished year), "of" yearly "books this year", yearly
if count(finished year) >= should
  "Ahead of pace by" count(finished year) - should head color good
else
  "Behind pace by" should - count(finished year) head color warn
section "Per month", "average rating" round(avg(finished.rating) * 10) / 10
  share finished year by month, count(it), it.key
list finished top 5, "No books finished yet this year."

        "In the lead" small
