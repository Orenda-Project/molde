needs: add a trip with route, passengers and fare | add an expense with kind and amount | collection minus expenses today | bar chart of 7 days collection | passengers carried today
---
app "Conductor Cash" bus grey
table run route pick A|B|C = A, riders num 1..120, fare money
table cost what pick Fuel|Tea|Repair|Other = Fuel, amount money
topbar run, "Add trip"
row gap 2
  statcard sum(run.fare today) - sum(cost.amount today), "left after expenses today"
  statcard sum(run.riders today), "passengers today"
section "Collection", "last 7 days"
  daybars last 7 days, sum(run.fare on it)
each run today sort at desc as r empty "No trips yet today."
  row card gap 2 tap edit r swipe remove r
    "Route" r.route bold grow
    r.riders "riders" faint
    r.fare bold
form cost, "Add expense"
