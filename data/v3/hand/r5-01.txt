asthma peak flow: I blow into the meter morning and night and write the number. my best is 450. green zone is 80% of best or above, yellow 50 to 80, red below 50. show the colour of the latest reading, a line chart and how many puffs of the reliever I used each day
needs: add a peak flow reading | colour zone against personal best | line chart of readings | log reliever puffs | puffs used today
---
app "Peak flow" cloud mint
table pf lpm num 50..800 unit "L/min", slot pick Morning|Night = Morning
table puff n num 1..10 = 1
setting best num = 450 "Personal best (L/min)"
let zone(v num) = if(v >= best * 0.8, good, if(v >= best * 0.5, warn, bad))
topbar "Peak flow", pf, "Add reading"
row card gap 2 fill zone(last(pf.lpm))
  stack grow gap 0
    last(pf.lpm) huge
    if(last(pf.lpm) >= best * 0.8, "Green zone", if(last(pf.lpm) >= best * 0.5, "Yellow zone: take care", "Red zone: use your plan")) bold
  round(last(pf.lpm) * 100 / best) "% of best" faint
section "Last 14 days"
  trend pf last 14 days sort at, it.lpm
section "Reliever puffs", "today" sum(puff.n today)
  button "+1 puff" tap add puff n = 1
  daybars last 7 days, sum(puff.n on it)

@rich domain=r5_mood_checkin topic=mood check-in chips style=custom_ui
a quick mood check-in for my mental health. tap one of five faces for how I feel, write one line if I want, and show the week as dots, my average mood and the days I felt low (1 or 2) this month
needs: tap a mood from 1 to 5 | optional note | week dots for check-ins | average mood this week | low days this month
---
app "Mood" smile pink
table mood score num 1..5, note text?
state pick num = 3
let lbl = if(pick = 1, "Very low", if(pick = 2, "Low", if(pick = 3, "Okay", if(pick = 4, "Good", "Great"))))
if(count(mood today) > 0, "Checked in today", "How are you right now?") head
row gap 1
  each 1..5
    button it fill if(pick = it, accent, soft) grow tap set pick = it
lbl faint
button "Save check-in" fill accent tap add mood score = pick
weekdots count(mood on it) > 0
row gap 2
  statcard round(avg(mood.score week) * 10) / 10, "average this week", 5
  statcard count(days month, avg(mood.score on it) <= 2 and count(mood on it) > 0), "low days this month"
section "Mood, last 30 days"
  trend mood last 30 days sort at, it.score
list mood by day, "No check-ins yet."
