@rich domain=r6_car_service_km topic=car service by kilometres style=detailed
the family car should get an oil change every 5000 km but we only remember when it makes noise. enter the odometer, log each service with km, what was done and cost, and show how many km until the next oil change
needs: setting for the current odometer | log a service with km, type and cost | km left until next oil change | total spent on service this year | list of services
---
app "Car service" car grey
table service km num 0.., what pick Oil|Tyres|Brakes|Battery|Other = Oil, cost money
setting odo num = 0 "Odometer now (km)"
setting gap_km num = 5000 "Oil change every (km)"
let next = last(service.km where what = Oil) + gap_km - odo
topbar "Car service", service, "Log service"
statcard next, "km until the next oil change", gap_km
if next < 500
  "Oil change is close. Book it." bold color warn
statcard sum(service.cost year), "spent on the car this year"
list service sort km desc, "No services logged yet. Add the last oil change."
