---

statcard count(arrival week where came > 8:30), "late arrivals this week"

      "On time" color good bold

@rich domain=flight_delays topic=flight delay log style=multi_feature
i fly a lot for work and want to log each flight with the planned and actual departure times, to see the delay in minutes, the average delay, how many flights left more than 15 minutes late and a bar chart of delay per flight
needs: log a flight with route, planned and actual departure | delay in minutes per flight | average delay | count of flights over 15 minutes late | bar chart of delay per flight

app "Flight Delays" plane blue dark
table flight route text, planned time, actual time
let delay(f flight) = (f.actual - f.planned) / 1m
form flight, "Log flight"
row gap 2

section "Delay per flight", "last 10"
  bars flight top 10, delay(it), it.route
each flight top 8 as f empty "No flights logged yet."
  row card gap 2 tap edit f swipe remove f
    stack grow gap 0
      f.route bold
      f.planned "planned" f.actual "actual" faint
    delay(f) "min" bold color if(delay(f) > 15, bad, ink)
