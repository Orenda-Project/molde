planning a wedding dinner. number of guests, price per head for the menu i choose (basic, standard or premium), a fixed cost for tents and waiters, and 10% service tax. show the full cost and per head with everything
needs: number of guests | pick basic, standard or premium menu | fixed cost for tents and waiters | service tax | total cost and cost per head
---
app "Dinner Cost" food blue
state guests num = 200
state menu pick Basic|Standard|Premium = Standard
state fixed money = 150000
let perhead = if(menu = Basic, 1200, if(menu = Standard, 1800, 2600))
let food = guests * perhead
let tax = (food + fixed) * 0.1
field guests "Guests"
choice menu
field fixed "Tents, waiters and decoration"
if guests > 0
  statcard round(food + fixed + tax), "total dinner cost"
  row gap 2
    stat round((food + fixed + tax) / guests), "per guest in total"
    stat food, "food at" perhead "each"
  "Tax 10%:" round(tax) faint
else
  "Type the number of guests to cost the dinner." faint
