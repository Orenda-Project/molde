wedding has mehndi, barat and walima, each with its own guest count and cost per head. add each event, show cost of each and the total, against my budget with a bar for each event
needs: add event with guests and cost per head | cost of each event | total | budget progress | share bars per event
---
app "Wedding events" gift orange
table event name text, guests num 1..2000, per_head money
setting budget money = 2000000 "Total budget"
let total = sum(event, it.guests * it.per_head)
topbar "Wedding events", event, "Add event"
goal total, budget, "planned cost"
if total > budget
  "Over budget by" total - budget color bad bold
section "Cost by event"
  share event, it.guests * it.per_head, it.name
each event sort at as e empty "No events yet. Add mehndi, barat and walima."
  row card gap 2 tap edit e swipe remove e
    stack grow gap 0
      e.name bold
      e.guests "guests at" e.per_head faint
    e.guests * e.per_head bold

@rich domain=c2_kameti_committee topic=committee_payout_turns style=detailed
we run a monthly kameti. number of members, amount each pays a month, and my turn number. tell me the payout, how much I will have paid by my turn, and whether early or late turn is better for me
needs: members and monthly amount | my turn number slider | payout amount | paid before my turn | gain or loss
---
app "Kameti" users purple
state members num = 12
state amount money = 5000
state turn num = 6
let pot = members * amount
let paid_in = amount * turn
let gain = pot - paid_in
section "The committee"
  "Members" members faint
  slider members 3..30
  field amount "Each pays a month"
  "My turn" turn "of" members faint
  slider turn 1..30
statcard pot, "payout in month" min(turn, members)
row gap 2
  stat round(amount * min(turn, members)), "I will have paid by then"
  stat round(pot - amount * min(turn, members)), "difference"
if turn <= 1
  "Early turn: it works like an interest free loan." faint
else
  if turn >= members
    "Last turn: you lend the others all year." faint
  else
    "A middle turn is neutral." faint
