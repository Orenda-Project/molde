@rich domain=c2_zakat_shares_portfolio topic=zakat_on_stock_holdings style=multi_feature
I hold some PSX shares and zakat is due on their market value. let me add each holding with number of shares and today's price, add cash, check nisab against silver rate, and show zakat and which holding is biggest
needs: add holding with shares and price | cash setting | silver rate for nisab | zakat due at 2.5% on total | share chart of holdings
---
app "Share zakat" chart ink
table holding name text, shares num 1.., price money
setting cash money = 0 "Cash and bank"
setting silver money = 0 "Silver price per gram"
let stocks = sum(holding, it.shares * it.price)
let wealth = stocks + cash
let nisab = silver * 612.36
topbar "Share zakat", holding, "Add holding"
statcard stocks, "value of shares"
if nisab = 0
  "Set the silver price in the settings to check the nisab." faint tap edit silver
else
  if wealth >= nisab
    statcard round(wealth * 0.025), "zakat due at 2.5% on" round(wealth)
  else
    "Below the nisab of" round(nisab) ", no zakat due." color good bold
section "Biggest holdings"
  share holding sort shares * price desc top 5, it.shares * it.price, it.name
list holding top 6, "No shares yet. Add each company with the shares you own."
