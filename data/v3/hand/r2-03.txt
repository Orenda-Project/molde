i deliver food on a bike. after each drop i enter the restaurant, the fare, the tip and the kilometres. show today's earnings against my target, earnings for the last week, and how much i earn per kilometre
needs: log a drop with restaurant, fare, tip and km | today's earnings against daily target | last 7 days earnings chart | earnings per kilometre | list of today's drops
---
app "Rider Log" bike ocean dark
table drop restaurant text, fare money, tip money = 0, km num 0.1..60 = 3
setting target money = 3000 "Daily target"
let today_total = sum(drop.fare today) + sum(drop.tip today)
topbar "Rider Log", drop, "Log a drop"
goal today_total, target, "earned today"
row gap 2
  statcard count(drop today), "drops today"
  statcard round((sum(drop.fare week) + sum(drop.tip week)) / max(sum(drop.km week), 1)), "per km this week"
section "Last 7 days", "line is your target"
  goalbars last 7 days, sum(drop.fare on it) + sum(drop.tip on it), target
list drop today, "No drops yet today."

@rich domain=r2_restaurant_diary topic=restaurant_visits_diary style=detailed
i like to remember the restaurants we try. add each visit with the place, cuisine, what the bill was, stars and whether i would go again. filter by cuisine, show how much i spent eating out this month and my average rating
needs: add a visit with place, cuisine, bill, stars and would go again | filter by cuisine | spent eating out this month | average rating | list of places to go again
---
app "Eating Out" star plum
table visit place text, cuisine pick Desi|Chinese|Fast_food|Cafe|Other = Desi, bill money, stars num 1..5 = 4, again bool
state want pick All|Desi|Chinese|Fast_food|Cafe|Other = All
topbar "Eating Out", visit, "Add a visit"
row gap 2
  statcard sum(visit.bill month), "spent eating out this month"
  statcard round(avg(visit.stars) * 10) / 10, "average stars"
choice want
each visit where want = All or cuisine = want sort stars desc as v empty "No visits here yet. Add the last place you ate."
  row card gap 2 tap edit v swipe remove v
    stack grow gap 0
      v.place bold
      v.cuisine "·" v.stars "stars ·" v.bill faint
    toggle v.again
