@rich domain=r4_school_book_club topic=school_book_club_meetings style=multi_feature
i run the book club at our school. each meeting has the book, author, who hosts and the date. after the meeting we rate the book out of 5 and i keep a few discussion questions for the next one. show the next meeting, the ratings so far and the average
needs: add a meeting with book, author, host and date | shows the next meeting | rate a book out of 5 after the meeting | discussion questions for a meeting | average rating of all books
---
app "Book Club" book ink dark
table meeting book text, author text?, host text, held date, score num? 1..5
table ask meeting ref meeting, q text
let nxt = nth(meeting where held >= today sort held, 1)
topbar "Book Club", meeting, "Add meeting"
stack card gap 1
  "Next meeting" faint
  nxt.book head
  nxt.held "hosted by" nxt.host faint
  each nxt.ask as a empty "No questions yet. Add some below."
    "-" a.q
statcard round(avg(meeting.score) * 10) / 10, "average rating", 5
section "Add a question"
  form ask, "Save question"
section "Rated books"
  each meeting where held < today sort held desc as m empty "No past meetings yet."
    row gap 2 tap edit m swipe remove m
      m.book bold grow
      m.score "/ 5" faint
