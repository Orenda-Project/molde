@create style=detailed domain=a3_grocery_stores
Compare prices of the same item at two shops. I add the item with a price at Shop A and Shop B and the app tells me which is cheaper and how much I save in total if I buy everything at the cheaper one.
---
app "Price compare" cart mint
table cmp item text, a money, b money
let save = sumof(cmp, abs(it.a - it.b))
"Savings by picking the cheaper" save big color good
each cmp sort item as c
  row card gap 2 tap edit c swipe remove c
    c.item bold grow
    "A" c.a color if(c.a <= c.b, good, ink)
    "B" c.b color if(c.b < c.a, good, ink)
form cmp, "Add item"
===
