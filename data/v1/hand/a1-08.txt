@create style=multi_feature domain=a1_walk_club
walking club log: km walked, a chart of the last 8 weeks with axes, total this month, and a message when the list is empty
---
app "Walking Club" walk mint
table walk km num 0.2..60 unit "km"
text fixed(sum(walk.km month), 1) "km this month" big
plot 0..8, 0..max(maxof(1..8, sum(walk.km week of (today - 7 * (8 - it)))), 10) axes gridlines
  each 1..8
    rect index + 0.15, 0, 0.7, sum(walk.km week of (today - 7 * (8 - it))) fill accent radius 2
    tick index + 0.5, "w" it
form walk
each walk sort at desc top 5 as w empty "No walks yet"
  row card tap edit w swipe remove w
    w.km "km" bold grow
    w.at faint
===
@create style=detailed domain=a1_cal_week_plot
Calories per day for the week as a chart with a gridline, and my daily limit setting. Show today's number big and turn it orange if I am over.
---
app "Calorie Week" apple ocean
table meal item text, kcal num 0..3000
setting limit num = 2000 "Daily limit"
let t = sum(meal.kcal today)
text grouped(t) "kcal" big color if(t > limit, warn, good)
plot 0..7, 0..max(limit, maxof(days last 7 days, sum(meal.kcal on it))) axes gridlines
  each days last 7 days
    rect index + 0.15, 0, 0.7, sum(meal.kcal on it) fill if(sum(meal.kcal on it) > limit, warn, accent) radius 2
    tick index + 0.5, dayname(it)
form meal
===
