@create style=detailed domain=a2_aid_families
We distribute aid to families in villages. Keep each family with the number of members and village, tap what we gave (ration, medicine, clothes or cash), and show the families who got nothing in the last 30 days first.
---
app "Aid families" heart mint
table family head_name text, members num 1..20, village text?
table pack family ref family, what pick Ration|Medicine|Clothes|Cash = Ration
countof(family, due(it.pack, 30)) "families without help for 30 days" big
each family sort since(it.pack) desc as f empty "No families yet"
  stack card gap 1 tap edit f
    row
      f.head_name bold grow
      f.village faint
    if due(f.pack, 30)
      "Needs a visit" size small color warn
    row gap 1
      each options pack.what as w
        button w tap add pack family = f, what = w
form family, "Add family"
