@create style=detailed domain=a1_quit_smoking
I am quitting smoking. I set the quit date, how many cigarettes I smoked a day and the price of one. Show days clean, money saved, and a button when I resist a craving.
---
app "Quit Smoking" fire slate
table smoke
table craving
setting start date "Quit date"
setting before num = 15 "Cigarettes a day before"
setting price money = 30 "Price of one cigarette"
let days = today - start
stat days, "days since I quit"
stat (before * days - count(smoke)) * price, "money saved so far"
stat count(craving), "cravings resisted"
button "I resisted a craving" tap add craving
button "I smoked one" tap add smoke
"Smoked since quit date" count(smoke) faint
===
