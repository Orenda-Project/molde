@askset class=cannot_do
send a whatsapp to my customers when their order is ready
ask: Molde apps cannot send messages to other people. Do you want an order list where you tick ready orders, so you can message them yourself? | Yes, an order list | No, never mind
plan: # need: cannot message, field customer, field item, tick, list
---
app "Orders" bag orange
table order customer text, item text, ready bool
count(order where ready = yes) "ready to send" head
each order where ready = no as o
  row card gap 2 tap edit o swipe remove o
    toggle o.ready
    stack grow gap 0
      o.customer bold
      o.item faint
"Ready" faint
list order where ready = yes
form order, "Add order"
===
