Zakat and tip, in the slate colours.
You can change: "Cash and bank": money, starts at 0; "Gold (grams)": number, starts at 0; "Silver (grams)": number, starts at 0; "Gold price per gram": money, starts at 0; "Silver price per gram": money, starts at 0; "Debts due now": money, starts at 0.
Works out:
- wealth is cash plus gold times gold rate plus silver times silver rate minus debts.
- nisab is 612.36 times silver rate.
Remembers while you use it: bill, starting at 0; tip, starting at 10; people, starting at 1.
Shows:
- a screen "Zakat":
  - a box to fill in cash.
  - a box to fill in gold.
  - a box to fill in silver.
  - a box to fill in gold rate.
  - a box to fill in silver rate.
  - a box to fill in debts.
  - text: "Zakat due (2.5%)" when wealth is at least nisab, otherwise "Below nisab: no zakat due".
  - text: wealth times 0.025 when wealth is at least nisab, otherwise 0 rounded.
- a screen "Tip":
  - a box to fill in bill, labelled "Bill".
  - text: "Tip", tip, "%".
  - a slider for tip from 0 to 30.
  - text: "People", people.
  - a slider for people from 1 to 20.
  - text: "Tip", bill times tip divided by 100 rounded.
  - text: "Each pays", bill times the result of 1 plus tip divided by 100 divided by people rounded.
