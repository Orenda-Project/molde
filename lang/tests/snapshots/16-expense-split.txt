Split (icon users, theme ocean).
Keeps:
- friend: name (text). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- cost: what (text), amount (money), paid (a friend). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- share = total amount of cost / number of friend.
Shows:
- text "Total" [total amount of cost] "," [share] "each".
- for friend:
  - text [name of f].
  - text ["gets back" if bal >= 0, else "owes"] [abs(bal)].
- a form to add a friend.
- a form to add a cost.
- a list of cost (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
