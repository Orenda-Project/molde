Sleep (icon moon, theme ink).
Keeps:
- night: slept (time of day), woke (time of day), quality (number, 1 to 5, starts at 3). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Sleep goal" (duration, starts at 8 h).
Works out:
- avg7 = sum of its woke - its slept + 24 h if its woke < its slept, else its woke - its slept over night in the last 7 days / number of night in the last 7 days.
Shows:
- text "Average sleep, last 7 nights".
- text [avg7].
- a bar chart of sum of its woke - its slept + 24 h if its woke < its slept, else its woke - its slept / 1 h over night on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- text "Average quality" [average quality of night in the last 7 days rounded] "of 5".
- a form to add a night.
- a list of night grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
Reminder "Time to wind down" at 22:30.
