Sleep, in the ink colours.
Keeps:
- night: slept: time of day; woke: time of day; quality: number, 1 to 5, starts at 3. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Sleep goal": length of time, starts at 8 hours.
Works out:
- avg7 is the sum of its woke minus its slept plus 24 hours when its woke is less than its slept, otherwise its woke minus its slept over the night in the last 7 days divided by how many night entries in the last 7 days.
Shows:
- text: "Average sleep, last 7 nights".
- text: avg7.
- a bar chart of the sum of its woke minus its slept plus 24 hours when its woke is less than its slept, otherwise its woke minus its slept divided by 1 hour over the night on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- text: "Average quality", the average quality of night in the last 7 days rounded, "of 5".
- a form to add a night.
- a list of night by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
Reminder "Time to wind down" at 22:30 every day.
