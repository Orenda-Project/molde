Tasbeeh, in the mint colours.
Keeps:
- zikr: phrase: one of SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, AllahuAkbar, starts at SubhanAllah. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Count to": number, starts at 33.
Works out:
- n is how many zikr entries today where phrase is ph.
Remembers while you use it: ph, starting at SubhanAllah.
Shows:
- for each choice of the phrase of the zikr:
  - a button the choice; a tap sets ph to the choice.
- a drawing made of circles, arcs, labels; a tap adds a zikr with phrase set to ph.
- when n is more than 0 and n leaves over when divided by target is 0, it shows the notice "Round complete".
- text: "Tap the circle to count.", how many zikr entries today, "today in all.".
