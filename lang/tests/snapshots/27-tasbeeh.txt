Tasbeeh (icon beads, theme mint).
Keeps:
- zikr: phrase (one of SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, AllahuAkbar, starts at SubhanAllah). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Count to" (number, starts at 33).
Works out:
- n = number of zikr today where phrase = ph.
Remembers while you use it: ph (starts at "SubhanAllah").
Shows:
- for each option of phrase of zikr:
  - a button [the item]; tap sets ph to the item.
- a drawing (circle, arc, label); tap adds a zikr (phrase = ph).
- when n > 0 and n % target = 0: shows a notice "Round complete".
- text "Tap the circle to count." [number of zikr today] "today in all.".
