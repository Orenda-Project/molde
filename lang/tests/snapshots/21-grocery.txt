Groceries, in the mint colours.
Keeps:
- item: name: text; qty: text, optional; got: yes or no. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text: how many item entries where got is no, "to buy".
- for each item where got is no:
  - a group; a swipe removes the item, with undo:
    - a tick box for the got of the item.
    - text: the name of the item, the qty of the item.
- text: "In the cart".
- for each item where got is yes:
  - a group; a swipe removes the item, with undo:
    - a tick box for the got of the item.
    - text: the name of the item.
- a form to add a item.
