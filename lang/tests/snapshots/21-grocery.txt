Groceries (icon cart, theme mint).
Keeps:
- item: name (text), qty (text, optional), got (yes/no). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text [number of item where got = no] "to buy".
- for item where got = no:
  - a row; swipe removes i (with undo):
    - a tick box for got of i.
    - text [name of i] [qty of i].
- text "In the cart".
- for item where got = yes:
  - a row; swipe removes i (with undo):
    - a tick box for got of i.
    - text [name of i].
- a form to add a item.
