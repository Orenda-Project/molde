Weight (icon scale, theme mint).
Keeps:
- weigh: kg (number, 20 to 300). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Target weight (kg)" (number, starts at 70).
Works out:
- kg = latest kg of weigh.
Shows:
- text [kg] "kg".
- text [abs(kg - target rounded)] "kg to target," [kg - first kg of weigh in the last 30 days rounded] "kg in 30 days".
- a line chart of its kg over weigh in the last 90 days sorted by at.
- a form to add a weigh.
- a list of weigh grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
