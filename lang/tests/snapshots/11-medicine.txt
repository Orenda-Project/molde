Medicine, in the plum colours.
Keeps:
- med: name: text; dose: text, optional; time: time of day. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- taken: med: a med. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text: how many taken entries today, "of", how many med entries, "doses taken today".
- for each med sorted by time:
  - a group; a tap opens the med for editing; a swipe removes the med, with undo:
    - text: its name.
    - text: its time, its dose.
    - a tick box for its taken.
- a form to add a med.
Reminder "Time for your medicine" on each time of the med entries.
