Medicine (icon pill, theme plum).
Keeps:
- med: name (text), dose (text, optional), time (time of day). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- taken: med (a med). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text [number of taken today] "of" [number of med] "doses taken today".
- for med sorted by time:
  - a row; tap opens the item for editing; swipe removes the item (with undo):
    - text [its name].
    - text [its time] [its dose].
    - a tick box for its taken.
- a form to add a med.
Reminder "Time for your medicine" at each time of med.
