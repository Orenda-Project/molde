Exercise (icon dumbbell, theme ink).
Keeps:
- sets: exercise (text), kg (number, at least 0, optional), reps (number, at least 1). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: ex (starts at latest exercise of sets).
Shows:
- text [number of sets today] "sets today," [sum of its kg * its reps over sets today] "kg moved".
- a bar chart of number of sets on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a form to add a sets.
- a button "Repeat last set"; tap adds a sets (exercise = latest exercise of sets, kg = latest kg of sets, reps = latest reps of sets).
- a countdown timer of 90 s ("Rest"); when it ends: shows a notice "Time is up".
- an input for ex ("Progress for").
- a line chart of its kg over sets where exercise = ex sorted by at.
- a list of sets grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
