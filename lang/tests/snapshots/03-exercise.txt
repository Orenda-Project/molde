Exercise, in the ink colours.
Keeps:
- sets: exercise: text; kg: number, at least 0, optional; reps: number, at least 1. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: ex, starting at the latest exercise of sets.
Shows:
- text: how many sets entries today, "sets today,", the sum of its kg times its reps over the sets today, "kg moved".
- a bar chart of how many sets entries on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a form to add a sets.
- a button "Repeat last set"; a tap adds a sets with exercise set to the latest exercise of sets, kg set to the latest kg of sets, reps set to the latest reps of sets.
- a countdown timer of 90 seconds, labelled "Rest"; when it ends it shows the notice "Time is up".
- a box to fill in ex, labelled "Progress for".
- a line chart of its kg over sets where exercise is ex sorted by at.
- a list of sets by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
