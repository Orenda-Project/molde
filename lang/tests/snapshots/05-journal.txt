Journal, in the plum colours.
Keeps:
- entry: mood: number, 1 to 5; note: long text. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: d, a new entry being filled in; q, starting at "".
Shows:
- for "awful", "low", "okay", "good", "great":
  - a button the value; a tap sets the mood of the d to its position plus 1.
- a box to fill in the note of the d, labelled "What is on your mind?".
- a button "Save entry"; a tap saves the new d.
- text: the current day streak of entry entries, "day streak,", how many entry entries in the last 7 days, "entries in 7 days".
- a heat map of how many entry entries on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- text: "Mood, last 30 days. Average", the average mood of entry in the last 30 days rounded.
- a line chart of its mood over entry in the last 30 days sorted by at.
- a box to fill in q, labelled "Search entries".
- a list of entry where note contains q by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
