Stock (icon package, theme slate).
Keeps:
- item: name (text), qty (number, at least 0), low (number, at least 0, starts at 5), price (money, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- short = how many of item have its qty <= its low.
Shows:
- text [short] "items running low".
- for item sorted by qty:
  - a row; tap opens i for editing; swipe removes i (with undo):
    - text [name of i].
    - a button "-"; tap sets qty of i to qty of i - 1.
    - text [qty of i].
    - a button "+"; tap sets qty of i to qty of i + 1.
- text "Stock value" [sum of its qty * its price over item].
- a form to add a item.
