Stock, in the slate colours.
Keeps:
- item: name: text; qty: number, at least 0; low: number, at least 0, starts at 5; price: money, optional. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- short is how many of the item have its qty is at most its low.
Shows:
- text: short, "items running low".
- for each item sorted by qty:
  - a group; a tap opens the item for editing; a swipe removes the item, with undo:
    - text: the name of the item.
    - a button "-"; a tap sets the qty of the item to the qty of the item minus 1.
    - text: the qty of the item.
    - a button "+"; a tap sets the qty of the item to the qty of the item plus 1.
- text: "Stock value", the sum of its qty times its price over the item.
- a form to add a item.
