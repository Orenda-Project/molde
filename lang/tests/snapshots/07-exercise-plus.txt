Exercise, in the ink colours.
Keeps:
- lift: exercise: text; kg: number, at least 0, starts at 0; sets: number, at least 1, starts at 3; reps: number, at least 1. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- vol is the sum of its kg times its sets times its reps over the lift this week.
- before is the sum of its kg times its sets times its reps over the lift last week.
Remembers while you use it: ex, starting at the latest exercise of lift.
Shows:
- a big number: vol, labelled "kg moved this week".
- a big number: the result of vol minus before times 100 divided by before rounded, labelled "% vs last week".
- a big number: how many of the day this week have how many lift entries on that day is more than 0, labelled "days trained".
- a form to add a lift.
- text: "Use 0 kg for bodyweight moves like push-ups.".
- a button "Repeat last"; a tap adds a lift with exercise set to the latest exercise of lift, kg set to the latest kg of lift, sets set to the latest sets of lift, reps set to the latest reps of lift.
- text: "New records, last 30 days".
- for each lift in the last 30 days sorted by kg, highest first:
  - if the kg of the lift is more than 0 and the kg of the lift is the highest kg of lift where exercise is the exercise of the lift:
    - text: the exercise of the lift, the kg of the lift, "kg".
- a box to fill in ex, labelled "Progress for".
- a line chart of its kg over lift where exercise is ex sorted by at.
- text: "Best", the highest kg of lift where exercise is ex, "kg".
- a bar chart of how many lift entries on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a list of lift by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
