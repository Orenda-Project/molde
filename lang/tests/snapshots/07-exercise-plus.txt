Exercise (icon dumbbell, theme ink).
Keeps:
- lift: exercise (text), kg (number, at least 0, starts at 0), sets (number, at least 1, starts at 3), reps (number, at least 1). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- vol = sum of its kg * its sets * its reps over lift this week.
- before = sum of its kg * its sets * its reps over lift last week.
Remembers while you use it: ex (starts at latest exercise of lift).
Shows:
- a big number: vol ("kg moved this week").
- a big number: (vol - before) * 100 / before rounded ("% vs last week").
- a big number: how many of each day this week have number of lift on that day > 0 ("days trained").
- a form to add a lift.
- text "Use 0 kg for bodyweight moves like push-ups.".
- a button "Repeat last"; tap adds a lift (exercise = latest exercise of lift, kg = latest kg of lift, sets = latest sets of lift, reps = latest reps of lift).
- text "New records, last 30 days".
- for lift in the last 30 days sorted by kg (highest first):
  - if kg of r > 0 and kg of r = highest kg of lift where exercise = exercise of r:
    - text [exercise of r] [kg of r] "kg".
- an input for ex ("Progress for").
- a line chart of its kg over lift where exercise = ex sorted by at.
- text "Best" [highest kg of lift where exercise = ex] "kg".
- a bar chart of number of lift on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a list of lift grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
