Journal (icon book, theme plum).
Keeps:
- entry: mood (number, 1 to 5), note (long text). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: d (a new entry being filled in), q (starts at "").
Shows:
- text "How are you feeling?".
- for "Awful", "Low", "Okay", "Good", "Great":
  - a stack; tap sets mood of d to its position + 1:
    - a drawing (circle, rect).
    - text [the item].
- an input for note of d ("What is on your mind?").
- a button "Save entry"; tap saves the new d.
- a big number: current day streak of entry ("day streak, including today" if number of entry today > 0, else "day streak; write today to keep it").
- a big number: how many of each day in the last 14 days have number of entry on that day > 0 ("of 14 days written").
- for each day in the last 14 days:
- text "Mood this week" [average mood of entry this week rounded] "vs" [average mood of entry last week rounded] "last week".
- a line chart of its mood over entry in the last 30 days sorted by at.
- an input for q ("Search entries").
- if q != "" and number of entry where has(note, q) = 0:
  - text "Nothing found. Try a shorter word.".
- a list of entry where has(note, q) grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
- text "Saved on this device only.".
