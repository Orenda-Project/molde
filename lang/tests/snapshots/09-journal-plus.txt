Journal, in the plum colours.
Keeps:
- entry: mood: number, 1 to 5; note: long text. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: d, a new entry being filled in; q, starting at "".
Shows:
- text: "How are you feeling?".
- for "Awful", "Low", "Okay", "Good", "Great":
  - a group; a tap sets the mood of the d to its position plus 1:
    - a drawing made of circles, rectangles.
    - text: the value.
- a box to fill in the note of the d, labelled "What is on your mind?".
- a button "Save entry"; a tap saves the new d.
- a big number: the current day streak of entry entries, labelled "day streak, including today" when how many entry entries today is more than 0, otherwise "day streak; write today to keep it".
- a big number: how many of the day in the last 14 days have how many entry entries on that day is more than 0, labelled "of 14 days written".
- for each day in the last 14 days:
- text: "Mood this week", the average mood of entry this week rounded, "vs", the average mood of entry last week rounded, "last week".
- a line chart of its mood over entry in the last 30 days sorted by at.
- a box to fill in q, labelled "Search entries".
- if q is not "" and how many entry entries where note contains q is 0:
  - text: "Nothing found. Try a shorter word.".
- a list of entry where note contains q by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
- text: "Saved on this device only.".
