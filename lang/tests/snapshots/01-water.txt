Water (icon drop, theme ocean).
Keeps:
- drink: ml (number, 1 to 5000, in ml). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Daily goal (ml)" (number, starts at 2000).
Works out:
- ml = total ml of drink today.
- p = clamp(ml / goal, 0, 1).
Shows:
- a drawing (rect, path).
- text [ml] "ml".
- text "Goal" [goal] "ml"; tap opens goal for editing.
- one-tap buttons that add a drink with ml set to each of 250, 500.
- a form to add a drink.
- a bar chart of total ml of drink on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a list of drink (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
