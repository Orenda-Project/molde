Water, in the ocean colours.
Keeps:
- drink: ml: number, 1 to 5000, in ml. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Daily goal (ml)": number, starts at 2000.
Works out:
- ml is the total ml of drink today.
- p is ml divided by goal kept between 0 and 1.
Shows:
- a drawing made of rectangles, outlines.
- text: ml, "ml".
- text: "Goal", goal, "ml"; a tap opens goal for editing.
- one-tap buttons that add a drink with ml set to each of 250, 500.
- a form to add a drink.
- a bar chart of the total ml of drink on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a list of drink; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
