Habits, in the mint colours.
Keeps:
- habit: name: text. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- check: habit: a habit. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- n is how many habit entries.
- done is how many check entries today.
Shows:
- a progress ring: done out of n, labelled "done today".
- text: "All done today" when done is n and n is more than 0, otherwise "Keep going".
- text: "Best streak", the highest of the current day streak of its check entries over the habit, "days".
- if n is 0:
  - text: "Start with one small thing you can do on a busy day.".
- for each habit:
  - a group; a tap opens the habit for editing; a swipe removes the habit, with undo:
    - text: the name of the habit.
    - text: the current day streak of the habit's check entries, "days".
    - a tick box for the check of the habit.
    - for each day in the last 7 days:
      - a group; a tap marks or unmarks the check of the habit on that day:
        - text: the weekday of the day.
- a big number: how many check entries this week times 100 divided by the result of n times the weekday number of today rounded, labelled "% this week".
- a big number: how many check entries in the last 30 days times 100 divided by the result of n times 30 rounded, labelled "% last 30 days".
- text: "Last 10 weeks".
- a grid of 7 columns:
  - for each day in the last 70 days:
- text: "Each row is a week. Darker means more habits done.".
- a form to add a habit.
