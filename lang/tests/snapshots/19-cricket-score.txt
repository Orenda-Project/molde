Cricket, in the ocean colours.
Keeps:
- ball: runs: number, 0 to 6, starts at 0; wide: yes or no; out: yes or no. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Overs": number, starts at 20.
Works out:
- legal is how many ball entries today where wide is no.
- runs is the total runs of ball today plus how many ball entries today where wide is yes.
- wkts is how many ball entries today where out is yes.
Shows:
- text: runs, "/", wkts.
- text: "Overs", legal divided by 6 rounded down plus legal leaves over when divided by 6 divided by 10, "of", overs, ". Run rate", runs times 6 divided by legal rounded.
- for 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6:
  - a button the value; a tap adds a ball with runs set to the value.
- a button "Wide"; a tap adds a ball with wide set to yes.
- a button "Out"; a tap adds a ball with out set to yes.
- a button "Undo"; a tap removes the latest ball entries today, with undo.
- text: "Last balls".
- for each ball today sorted by at, highest first, first 6:
  - a drawing made of circles, labels.
