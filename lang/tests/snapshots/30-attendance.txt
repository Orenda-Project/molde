Attendance (icon users, theme ocean).
Keeps:
- student: name (text). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- absent: student (a student). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: d (starts at today).
Shows:
- a button "<"; tap sets d to d - 1.
- text [dayname(d)] [monthday(d)] [monthname(d)].
- a button ">"; tap sets d to d + 1.
- text [number of student - number of absent on d] "of" [number of student] "present".
- a grid of 4 columns:
  - for student sorted by name:
    - a stack; tap marks or unmarks absent of s on d:
      - text [name of s].
      - text ["Absent" if number of absent of s on d > 0, else "Present"].
- text "Tap a name to mark absent. Everyone starts present.".
- for student sorted by name:
  - text [name of s].
  - text [number of absent of s in the month of d] "days absent this month".
- a form to add a student.
