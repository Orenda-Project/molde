Attendance, in the ocean colours.
Keeps:
- student: name: text. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- absent: student: a student. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: d, starting at today.
Shows:
- a button "<"; a tap sets d to d minus 1.
- text: the weekday of d, the day of the month of d, the month of d.
- a button ">"; a tap sets d to d plus 1.
- text: how many student entries minus how many absent entries on d, "of", how many student entries, "present".
- a grid of 4 columns:
  - for each student sorted by name:
    - a group; a tap marks or unmarks the absent of the student on d:
      - text: the name of the student.
      - text: "Absent" when how many the student's absent entries on d is more than 0, otherwise "Present".
- text: "Tap a name to mark absent. Everyone starts present.".
- for each student sorted by name:
  - text: the name of the student.
  - text: how many the student's absent entries in the month of d, "days absent this month".
- a form to add a student.
