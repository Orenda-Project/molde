Khata, in the slate colours.
Keeps:
- person: name: text; phone: text, optional. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- entry: person: a person; amount: money; kind: one of Gave, Got; note: text, optional. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text: "People owe you", the total amount of entry where kind is Gave minus the total amount of entry where kind is Got.
- for each person:
  - a group; a tap opens the person for editing:
    - text: the name of the person.
    - text: "owes you" when bal is at least 0, otherwise "you owe", bal without its sign.
    - text: "Last entry", the latest at of the person's entry entries.
- a form to add a person.
- a form to add a entry.
- a list of entry by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
