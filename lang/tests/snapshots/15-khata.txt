Khata (icon wallet, theme slate).
Keeps:
- person: name (text), phone (text, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- entry: person (a person), amount (money), kind (one of Gave, Got), note (text, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text "People owe you" [total amount of entry where kind = "Gave" - total amount of entry where kind = "Got"].
- for person:
  - a stack; tap opens p for editing:
    - text [name of p].
    - text ["owes you" if bal >= 0, else "you owe"] [abs(bal)].
    - text "Last entry" [latest at of p].
- a form to add a person.
- a form to add a entry.
- a list of entry grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
