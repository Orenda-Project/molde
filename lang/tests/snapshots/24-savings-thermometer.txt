Savings, in the mint colours.
Keeps:
- deposit: amount: money; note: text, optional. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Savings goal": money, starts at 100000.
Works out:
- total is the total amount of deposit.
- p is total divided by goal kept between 0 and 1.
Shows:
- a drawing made of rectangles, circles.
- text: total.
- text: p times 100 rounded, "% of", goal; a tap opens goal for editing.
- text: "This month", the total amount of deposit this month.
- one-tap buttons that add a deposit with amount set to each of 500, 1000, 5000.
- a form to add a deposit.
- a list of deposit by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
