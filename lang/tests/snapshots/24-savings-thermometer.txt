Savings (icon coin, theme mint).
Keeps:
- deposit: amount (money), note (text, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Savings goal" (money, starts at 100000).
Works out:
- total = total amount of deposit.
- p = clamp(total / goal, 0, 1).
Shows:
- a drawing (rect, circle).
- text [total].
- text [p * 100 rounded] "% of" [goal]; tap opens goal for editing.
- text "This month" [total amount of deposit this month].
- one-tap buttons that add a deposit with amount set to each of 500, 1000, 5000.
- a form to add a deposit.
- a list of deposit grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
