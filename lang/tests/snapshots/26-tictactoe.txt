Tic tac toe, in the ink colours.
Keeps:
- move: game: number; cell: number, 1 to 9; mark: one of X, O. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Game": number, starts at 1.
Works out:
- n is how many move entries where game is cur.
- turn is "X" when n leaves over when divided by 2 is 0, otherwise "O".
- xw is the result of how many of 123, 456, 789, 147, 258, 369, 159, 357 have how many move entries where game is cur and mark is "X" and the result of cell is the value divided by 100 rounded down or cell is the value divided by 10 rounded down leaves over when divided by 10 or cell is the value leaves over when divided by 10 is 3 is more than 0.
- ow is the result of how many of 123, 456, 789, 147, 258, 369, 159, 357 have how many move entries where game is cur and mark is "O" and the result of cell is the value divided by 100 rounded down or cell is the value divided by 10 rounded down leaves over when divided by 10 or cell is the value leaves over when divided by 10 is 3 is more than 0.
Shows:
- if xw or ow:
  - text: "X" when xw, otherwise "O", "wins!".
- otherwise:
  - text: "Draw" when n is 9, otherwise turn, "" when n is 9, otherwise "to play".
- a grid of 3 columns:
  - for each number from 1 to 9:
    - if how many move entries where game is cur and cell is the number is 0 and not xw and not ow:
      - a group; a tap adds a move with game set to cur, cell set to the number, mark set to turn:
    - otherwise:
      - text: the latest mark of move where game is cur and cell is the number.
- a button "New game"; a tap sets cur to cur plus 1.
