Habits, in the mint colours.
Keeps:
- habit: name: text. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- check: habit: a habit. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- n is how many habit entries.
Shows:
- text: how many check entries today, "of", n, "done today".
- if n is 0:
  - text: "Nothing to track yet. Add one.".
- for each habit:
  - a group; a tap opens the habit for editing; a swipe removes the habit, with undo:
    - text: its name.
    - text: the current day streak of its check entries, "day streak,", how many its check entries in the last 30 days times 100 divided by 30 rounded, "% in 30 days".
    - a tick box for its check.
- a big number: how many check entries this week times 100 divided by the result of n times the weekday number of today rounded, labelled "% this week".
- a big number: how many check entries in the last 30 days times 100 divided by the result of n times 30 rounded, labelled "% in 30 days".
- a big number: the highest of the current day streak of its check entries over the habit, labelled "best streak".
- a heat map of how many check entries on that day for each day in the last 70 days.
- a form to add a habit.
