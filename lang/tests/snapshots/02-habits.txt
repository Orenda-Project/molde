Habits (icon check, theme mint).
Keeps:
- habit: name (text). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- check: habit (a habit). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- n = number of habit.
Shows:
- text [number of check today] "of" [n] "done today".
- if n = 0:
  - text "Nothing to track yet. Add one.".
- for habit:
  - a row; tap opens the item for editing; swipe removes the item (with undo):
    - text [its name].
    - text [current day streak of its check] "day streak," [number of its check in the last 30 days * 100 / 30 rounded] "% in 30 days".
    - a tick box for its check.
- a big number: number of check this week * 100 / (n * weekday(today)) rounded ("% this week").
- a big number: number of check in the last 30 days * 100 / (n * 30) rounded ("% in 30 days").
- a big number: highest of current day streak of its check over habit ("best streak").
- a heat map of number of check on that day for each day in the last 70 days.
- a form to add a habit.
