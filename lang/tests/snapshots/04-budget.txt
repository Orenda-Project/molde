Budget, in the slate colours.
Keeps:
- spend: what: text; amount: money; category: one of Food, Bills, Travel, Fun, Health. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Monthly budget": money, starts at 80000.
Works out:
- spent is the total amount of spend in the month of m.
Remembers while you use it: m, starting at today; cat, starting at "".
Shows:
- arrows that move m back and forward one month.
- text: "Over budget by" when spent is more than budget, otherwise "Left to spend", budget minus spent without its sign.
- spent out of budget with a progress bar, labelled "spent".
- text: "Safe to spend", the result of budget minus spent divided by the result of the number of days in the month of m minus the day of the month of today plus 1 rounded, "a day. Last month:", the total amount of spend last month.
- a drawing made of lines, a line through points.
- for each group of spend entries in the month of m by category:
  - a group; a tap sets cat to "" when cat is its key, otherwise its key:
    - text: its key.
    - text: the total amount of the group.
    - a drawing made of rectangles.
- a form to add a spend.
- a list of spend in the month of m where cat is "" or category is cat by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
