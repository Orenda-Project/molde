Budget (icon wallet, theme slate).
Keeps:
- spend: what (text), amount (money), category (one of Food, Bills, Travel, Fun, Health). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Monthly budget" (money, starts at 80000).
Works out:
- spent = total amount of spend in the month of m.
Remembers while you use it: m (starts at today), cat (starts at "").
Shows:
- arrows that move m back and forward one month.
- text ["Over budget by" if spent > budget, else "Left to spend"] [abs(budget - spent)].
- spent out of budget with a progress bar ("spent").
- text "Safe to spend" [(budget - spent) / (monthdays(m) - monthday(today) + 1) rounded] "a day. Last month:" [total amount of spend last month].
- a drawing (line, line through points).
- for spend in the month of m grouped by category:
  - a stack; tap sets cat to "" if cat = its key, else its key:
    - text [its key].
    - text [total its amount].
    - a drawing (rect).
- a form to add a spend.
- a list of spend in the month of m where cat = "" or category = cat grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
