Study, in the ocean colours.
Keeps:
- lesson: subject: one of Maths, English, Science, History, Art; day: one of Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri; start: time of day; mins: number, 15 to 180, starts at 45. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- studied: lesson: a lesson. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Shows:
- text: "Today".
- for each lesson where day is the weekday of today sorted by start:
  - text: its start, its subject.
  - a tick box for its studied.
- a grid of 5 columns:
  - for each choice of the day of the lesson:
    - text: the choice.
    - for each lesson where day is the choice sorted by start:
      - a group; a tap opens the lesson for editing:
        - text: the subject of the lesson.
        - text: the start of the lesson, the mins of the lesson, "min".
- a form to add a lesson.
