Quiz (icon star, theme plum).
Keeps:
- item: question (text), a (text), b (text), c (text), right (one of A, B, C). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
- answer: item (a item), chose (one of A, B, C). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- score = how many of answer have its chose = its item right.
Shows:
- text [score] "of" [number of answer] "right".
- for item:
  - if number of answer of x = 0:
    - text [question of x].
    - a button [a of x]; tap adds a answer (item = x, chose = "A").
    - a button [b of x]; tap adds a answer (item = x, chose = "B").
    - a button [c of x]; tap adds a answer (item = x, chose = "C").
- for answer:
  - text [item question of r].
  - text ["Right" if chose of r = item right of r, else "Wrong"].
- a form to add a item.
