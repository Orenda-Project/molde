Cycle, in the plum colours.
Keeps:
- period: flow: one of Light, Medium, Heavy, starts at Medium. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- cyc is the average days between period entries rounded.
Shows:
- text: "Day", days since the last of period entries plus 1, "of your cycle".
- text: "Next period in about", cyc minus days since the last of period entries, "days. Average cycle", cyc, "days.".
- a button "Period started today"; a tap adds a period.
- a month calendar of period; tap a day to mark or unmark it.
- a list of period by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
