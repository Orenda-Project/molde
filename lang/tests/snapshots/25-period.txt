Cycle (icon heart, theme plum).
Keeps:
- period: flow (one of Light, Medium, Heavy, starts at Medium). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Works out:
- cyc = average days between period rounded.
Shows:
- text "Day" [days since the last period + 1] "of your cycle".
- text "Next period in about" [cyc - days since the last period] "days. Average cycle" [cyc] "days.".
- a button "Period started today"; tap adds a period.
- a month calendar of period; tap a day to mark or unmark it.
- a list of period grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
