Focus (icon timer, theme ink).
Keeps:
- session: task (text, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Focus length" (duration, starts at 25 min).
Shows:
- a countdown timer of work ("Focus"); when it ends: adds a session, then shows a notice "Take a short break".
- a countdown timer of 5 min ("Break"); when it ends: shows a notice "Time is up".
- text [number of session today] "focus sessions today".
- a bar chart of number of session on that day for each day in the last 7 days.
- a list of session today (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
