Quran (icon book, theme mint).
Keeps:
- read: juz (number, 1 to 30), pages (number, at least 1). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Pages a day" (number, starts at 4).
Works out:
- done = total pages of read.
Shows:
- done out of 604 with a progress bar ("pages read").
- a grid of 10 columns:
  - for 1 to 30:
    - text [the item].
- text "Today" [total pages of read today] "of" [daily] "pages".
- a heat map of total pages of read on that day for each day in the last 28 days.
- text "At this pace you finish in" [(604 - done) * 30 / total pages of read in the last 30 days rounded] "days".
- a form to add a read.
- a list of read grouped by day (tap to edit, swipe to remove).
