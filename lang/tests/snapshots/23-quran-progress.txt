Quran, in the mint colours.
Keeps:
- read: juz: number, 1 to 30; pages: number, at least 1. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Pages a day": number, starts at 4.
Works out:
- done is the total pages of read.
Shows:
- done out of 604 with a progress bar, labelled "pages read".
- a grid of 10 columns:
  - for each number from 1 to 30:
    - text: the number.
- text: "Today", the total pages of read today, "of", daily, "pages".
- a heat map of the total pages of read on that day for each day in the last 28 days.
- text: "At this pace you finish in", the result of 604 minus done times 30 divided by the total pages of read in the last 30 days rounded, "days".
- a form to add a read.
- a list of read by day; tap one to edit it, swipe to remove it.
