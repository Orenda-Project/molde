Reading (icon book, theme plum).
Keeps:
- book: title (text), author (text, optional), status (one of Want, Reading, Done, starts at Want), finished (date, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: tab (starts at "Reading").
Shows:
- for each option of status of book:
  - a button [the item]; tap sets tab to the item.
- for book where status = tab sorted by title:
  - a row; tap opens b for editing; swipe removes b (with undo):
    - text [title of b].
    - text [author of b].
    - if status of b = "Reading":
      - a button "Finished"; tap sets status of b to "Done", then sets finished of b to today.
- a big number: number of book where status = "Done" and finished >= addmonths(today, -12) ("books finished in the last year").
- a form to add a book.
