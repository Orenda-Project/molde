Reading, in the plum colours.
Keeps:
- book: title: text; author: text, optional; status: one of Want, Reading, Done, starts at Want; finished: date, optional. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
Remembers while you use it: tab, starting at Reading.
Shows:
- for each choice of the status of the book:
  - a button the choice; a tap sets tab to the choice.
- for each book where status is tab sorted by title:
  - a group; a tap opens the book for editing; a swipe removes the book, with undo:
    - text: the title of the book.
    - text: the author of the book.
    - if the status of the book is Reading:
      - a button "Finished"; a tap sets the status of the book to Done, then sets the finished of the book to today.
- a big number: how many book entries where status is Done and finished is at least today moved by minus 12 months, labelled "books finished in the last year".
- a form to add a book.
