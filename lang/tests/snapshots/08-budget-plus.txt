Budget, in the slate colours.
Keeps:
- spend: amount: money; category: one of Food, Bills, Travel, Fun, Health, Other, starts at Food; note: text, optional. Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Monthly budget": money, starts at 80000.
Works out:
- spent is the total amount of spend in the month of m.
- day is the day of the month of today when the month of m is the month of today, otherwise the number of days in the month of m.
Remembers while you use it: m, starting at today; cat, starting at "".
Shows:
- arrows that move m back and forward one month.
- spent out of budget with a progress bar, labelled "spent so far".
- a big number: spent divided by day rounded, labelled "daily average".
- a big number: the result of budget minus spent divided by the result of the number of days in the month of m minus day plus 1 rounded, labelled "left per day".
- a big number: the total amount of spend last month where the day of the month of at is at most the day of the month of today, labelled "same point last month".
- text: "Spending through the month".
- a drawing made of lines, a line through points.
- text: "By category (tap to filter)".
- for each group of spend entries in the month of m by category:
  - a group; a tap sets cat to "" when cat is its key, otherwise its key:
    - text: its key.
    - text: the total amount of the group times 100 divided by spent rounded, "%".
    - text: the total amount of the group.
    - a drawing made of rectangles.
- a form to add a spend.
- for each group of spend entries in the month of m where cat is "" or category is cat by day:
  - text: the key of the group.
  - text: the total amount of the group.
  - for each the group:
    - a group; a tap opens the g for editing; a swipe removes the g, with undo:
      - text: the category of the g, the note of the g.
      - text: the amount of the g.
