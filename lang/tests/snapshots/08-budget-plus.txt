Budget (icon wallet, theme slate).
Keeps:
- spend: amount (money), category (one of Food, Bills, Travel, Fun, Health, Other, starts at Food), note (text, optional). Every entry also keeps when it was added.
You can change: "Monthly budget" (money, starts at 80000).
Works out:
- spent = total amount of spend in the month of m.
- day = monthday(today) if monthname(m) = monthname(today), else monthdays(m).
Remembers while you use it: m (starts at today), cat (starts at "").
Shows:
- arrows that move m back and forward one month.
- spent out of budget with a progress bar ("spent so far").
- a big number: spent / day rounded ("daily average").
- a big number: (budget - spent) / (monthdays(m) - day + 1) rounded ("left per day").
- a big number: total amount of spend last month where monthday(at) <= monthday(today) ("same point last month").
- text "Spending through the month".
- a drawing (line, line through points).
- text "By category (tap to filter)".
- for spend in the month of m grouped by category:
  - a stack; tap sets cat to "" if cat = its key, else its key:
    - text [its key].
    - text [total its amount * 100 / spent rounded] "%".
    - text [total its amount].
    - a drawing (rect).
- a form to add a spend.
- for spend in the month of m where cat = "" or category = cat grouped by day:
  - text [key of g].
  - text [total amount of g].
  - for g:
    - a row; tap opens r for editing; swipe removes r (with undo):
      - text [category of r] [note of r].
      - text [amount of r].
